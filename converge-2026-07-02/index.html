What this demonstrates

  • Legacy C4 is migrated in place to the unified renderer (replacement, not opt-in).
  • Both syntaxes converge on one visual language (RFC #7844) and share code (c4ShapeVocabulary, c4Labels, c4Legend, c4Palette).
  • Colours are theme-driven (legible across default/dark/forest/neutral).
